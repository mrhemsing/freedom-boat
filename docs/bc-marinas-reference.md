## Enrichment Notes

- Customs ports of entry worth a future data flag: Bedwell Harbour / Poets Cove,
  Sidney, Victoria, Nanaimo, Campbell River, and Prince Rupert.
- Harbour authorities and resort marinas usually take transient boaters; yacht
  clubs usually need member or reciprocal access and should be checked carefully.
- Fuel is high-value and inconsistently tagged in OSM. Treat OSM fuel values as
  hints until operator data confirms them.
