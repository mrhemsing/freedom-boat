# BC Off-Page SEO Playbook

Fair Tide's SEO moat is local specificity: server-rendered pages for BC places,
plus a small number of topical local links from boating, marina, harbour, yacht
club, sailing, and sport-fishing communities.

## Strategy

Win the long tail through the programmatic location layer:

- `[place] tides`
- `[place] tide times`
- `boating conditions [place] today`
- `marine forecast [place]`
- `[place] wind forecast`
- `public boat launch [place]`
- `[place] boat ramp`
- `[marina] guest moorage`
- `transient moorage [place]`
- `slack tide [pass]`

Win regional head terms through area hubs and local links:

- `/area/howe-sound`
- `/area/gulf-islands`
- `/area/salish-sea`

Every outreach target should be pointed to the most useful page, not always the
home page. If the contact is a harbour authority, link the relevant harbour or
nearby area hub. If the contact is a yacht club, link the closest marina,
launch, or route-planning area.

## Link Targets

Use these as the first URLs to promote:

- `https://fairtide.app/area/howe-sound`
- `https://fairtide.app/area/gulf-islands`
- `https://fairtide.app/area/salish-sea`
- `https://fairtide.app/marina/reed-point-marina-6`
- `https://fairtide.app/launch/vanier-park-boat-launch-1`
- `https://fairtide.app/plan-my-trip`

Rotate in specific marina and launch URLs as the outreach list expands. Deep
links are more natural than every mention pointing at the same page.

## Prospect Buckets

Prioritize links that are both local and boating-specific:

1. Harbour authorities and public harbour pages.
2. Yacht clubs and sailing clubs with resources, cruising, visitor, or reciprocal
   moorage pages.
3. BC boating and sport-fishing forums where helpful trip-planning resources are
   allowed.
4. Marina operators, chandleries, boatyards, yacht brokers, and charter
   businesses with local resource pages.
5. Regional marine directories and event organizations.
6. Safety, navigation, and boating education groups that publish resource lists.

Avoid generic paid directory spam. One honest link from a local boating resource
is worth more than many unrelated directory links.

## Outreach Rules

Keep the pitch useful and narrow:

- Lead with the local page that helps their audience.
- Mention live tides, wind, launch, and transient-moorage context.
- Do not ask for a generic SEO backlink.
- Do not mass-post the same wording across forums.
- If posting in a forum, answer a real question and disclose the connection.
- Prefer safety and trip-planning framing over product promotion.

## Measurement

Track progress weekly:

- New referring domains in Search Console.
- Indexed count for `/marina/*`, `/launch/*`, and `/area/*`.
- Queries with impressions for `[place] tides`, `[place] boating conditions`,
  `[place] launch`, and `[place] guest moorage`.
- Pages receiving external links.
- Area hub pages receiving impressions for broader BC boating terms.

Success target for the first pass: 10 to 20 relevant BC marine referring domains,
with at least half linking to deep marina, launch, or area pages.
